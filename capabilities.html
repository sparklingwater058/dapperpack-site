How we work

Everything a box needs, under one roof

Structural drawing, printing, foiling, embossing, laminating and gluing happen in our own workshop in Tangxia, Dongguan. That is the reason a 500-unit order is workable: nothing is passed to a second supplier with a minimum of its own.

01 — Structure

The dieline comes first

A box is decided by its drawing, not by its artwork. We draw the structure from your product, so the fit is settled before anyone talks about print.

Drawn from your product

Give us the product dimensions — a bottle, a jar, a folded garment, a candle — and we build the dieline around it, including the headspace and clearance it needs.

Or refined from yours

Already have a dieline? Send it and we will work from it, or adjust the structure where it does not suit the board or your packing line.

At no charge

Structural design and the dieline are free of charge, before you commit to a production run.

02 — Print & finish

Print, then make the surface do the work

Spot colour, matched to your reference

Send the reference you already work to — a printed sample, a swatch, a colour code — and we match the ink to it. Four-colour process printing for photographic artwork.

Foil, emboss, spot UV

Metallic and coloured foils, raised or debossed relief, and gloss highlights over a matt base. These three finishes do more to change how a box feels in the hand than any others.

Six laminations

Matt, gloss, soft-touch, anti-scratch, plastic-free and starlight — chosen against the artwork and how the box will be handled.

Recyclable board options

Where the product allows it, we will quote board and laminations that keep the pack recyclable — and tell you when a finish would work against that.

03 — Before production

Nothing goes on the press unapproved

  1. Dieline proof

    You see the structure and the dimensions first, and we change it until the fit is right.

  2. Sample

    White sample free, out in 7 days. A printed production sample is ready in 10 days, about US$100 per structure, refunded against orders over 10,000 units; the courier is on your account. Special materials and finishes can take longer.

  3. Your approval

    Structure, artwork, board and finish are signed off together — in writing, before the run starts.

  4. Production

    Printed, finished, glued and packed. Production lead time is 45 days from the later of sample approval and receipt of the deposit (30% deposit, balance by T/T); schedule and shipping terms are agreed with the quote, so there is nothing left to negotiate at the end.

The workshop

What we run, and how much of it

This is a working factory, not a sales office with a supplier list. One workshop in Tangxia, five product lines, and the presses below — so a run is scheduled, not outsourced and chased.

  • 11,000 m² — 7,000 m² of production and 4,000 m² of storage, in one building.
  • 150 people, including a separate sampling room with its own equipment.
  • Three offset presses — a 6-colour and a 5-colour Komori, plus a 2-colour press for spot work.
  • Two Fengchi flute laminators — offset-printed sheet laminated to corrugated board in house, so corrugated cartons are printed here, not bought in printed.
  • Peak season runs from June through January. If your launch falls in that window, book the slot early.

05 — Pricing

Why there is no price list

A box's price moves with the board, the size of the run and the finishing — the same carton in matt and in soft-touch foil are two different jobs. So every quote is built from the dieline.

  • Send: product dimensions, quantity, finishing you have in mind.
  • Get back: the quote and the dieline, within three working days.
  • Then: a white sample in 7 days, before you commit to a run.

What a quote covers

Included, and charged on top

Not knowing what else will appear on the invoice stops more first orders than the price itself. So here is the boundary before you ask.

Included

  • Structural design and the dieline — at no charge.
  • Printing and finishing to the specification we agree in writing.
  • Gluing, and packing the boxes for shipment.
  • The white sample itself — free of charge. A printed production sample is in 10 days, about US$100 per structure, refunded against orders over 10,000 units.

Charged separately

  • A printed production sample — about US$100 per structure, ready in 10 days, refunded against orders over 10,000 units. The fee does not include tooling.
  • Tooling and plate charges, where a job needs them — listed as their own lines on the quote.
  • Courier for samples — on your account.
  • Freight — quoted FOB Shenzhen unless you ask for another Incoterm.
Nothing else is added to the invoice without your agreement. If something would change the price after the quote — a board change, a different finish, a bigger run — you hear about it before it happens, not on the invoice.
