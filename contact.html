Faster quotes

What to put in the first email

  • The product and its dimensions — a photo next to a ruler works.
  • Quantity for the first run, even if it is a small one.
  • The structure if you know it, or leave it to us.
  • Finishing you are considering: lamination, foil, emboss, spot UV.
  • Artwork — ready, in progress, or nothing yet.
  • Where it will be sold, so we leave room for the labelling that market needs.

Missing some of that? Send what you have — the first reply is usually a question or two, not a quotation form.
