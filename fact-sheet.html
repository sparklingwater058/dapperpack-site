What we make

  • Folding cartons

    Printed paperboard, supplied flat and glued. Retail shelf and e-commerce.

  • Corrugated cartons

    Fluted board for fulfilment, multi-packs and export shipping. Printed or plain.

  • Rigid, book-style & drawer boxes

    Wrapped board, assembled: lid and base, hinged book-style, and sliding drawer.

The numbers

Minimum order
500 units per design and per size.
Dieline and structural design
At no charge, before you commit to a run.
Quote turnaround
Within three working days, with the dieline attached.
Sample
White sample free in 7 days; printed production sample US$100 in 10 days, refunded against orders over 10,000 units.
Production
Within 45 days of sample approval and a 30% deposit.
Finishing
Foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, spot colour matched to your reference; matt, gloss, soft-touch, anti-scratch, plastic-free and starlight lamination; recyclable board options.
Price list
None — every job is priced from its dieline, board and finishing. Ask and you will have it within three working days.
Monthly capacity
10 million folding cartons, 4 million rigid boxes, 1.5 million corrugated cartons, 1 million book-style and 500,000 drawer boxes a month.
