What we make

  • Folding cartons

    Printed paperboard, supplied flat and glued. Retail shelf and e-commerce.

  • Rigid & gift boxes

    Wrapped board, lid and base, assembled. Gift sets and limited editions.

  • Trays, sleeves & inserts

    Two-piece cartons and die-cut fitments that hold the product in place.

The numbers

Minimum order
500 units per design.
Dieline and structural design
At no charge, before you commit to a run.
Quote turnaround
Within one working day, with the dieline attached.
Sample
Free of charge; typically 7–10 days. Special materials and finishes can take longer. Courier is on your account.
Finishing
Foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, spot colour matched to your reference; matt, gloss, soft-touch, anti-scratch, plastic-free and starlight lamination; recyclable board options.
Printed prices
None — every job is priced from its dieline, board and finishing. Ask and you will have it in a day.

How to start

  1. Product dimensions

    Size and weight — a photo next to a ruler is fine.

  2. Quantity

    For the first run, even a small one.

  3. Finishing

    If you have one in mind; otherwise we will suggest.

No artwork needed to get a quote.

A quote covers

  • Structural design and the dieline.
  • Printing, finishing, gluing and packing.
  • The sample itself.

Charged separately

  • Tooling and plate charges, where a job needs them.
  • Courier for samples.
  • Freight — the shipping term is agreed with the quote.
