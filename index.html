What we make

Five product types cover most packaging briefs

Each one is drawn to your product dimensions, then printed and finished to the board and surface you choose.

Folding cartons

Printed paperboard cartons for retail shelf and e-commerce, in the board weight and finish your product needs. Supplied flat, glued at the side seam — erected and filled on your packing line.

Straight tuck, reverse tuck, crash-lock and sleeves — drawn to your dimensions.

Corrugated cartons

Fluted board for the part of the journey that is rough: fulfilment, multi-packs and export shipping. Printed or plain, die-cut on the same lines.

Single-wall and double-wall, matched to the weight being shipped.

Rigid & gift boxes

Set-up boxes — lid and base, or shoulder — with die-cut inserts — for limited editions, gift sets and anything where the unboxing carries the price point.

Lid and base in greyboard, wrapped in printed paper, with a die-cut insert inside.

Book-style boxes

A wrapped base with a hinged lid along one long edge — the format that makes a set feel like an object rather than a container.

One-piece wrap, hinged spine, optional magnet and ribbon pull.

Drawer boxes

An outer shell and an inner tray on a sliding fit. The fit is the whole job — set at the drawing stage, before any board is cut.

Ribbon or finger pull, a stop, and a die-cut insert inside.

How we work

From a dimension to a box, in five steps

  1. Send your dimensions

    Product size with any headspace you need, quantity and finishing. A sketch, a photograph or an existing dieline is fine.

  2. We draw the dieline

    Structural drawing at no charge, so the fit is checked before artwork and tooling.

  3. You get a quote

    Inside three working days, with the dieline attached so you can see what is being priced.

  4. Sample

    White sample free, out in 7 days. A printed production sample is in 10 days, about US$100 per structure, refunded against orders over 10,000 units. You cover the courier. Special materials and finishes can take longer.

  5. Production

    Schedule, packing and shipping terms (FOB Shenzhen unless you ask otherwise) are confirmed with the quote before anything goes on the press. Production lead time is 45 days from the later of sample approval and receipt of the deposit (30% deposit, balance by T/T).

Finishing & materials

The surface is where a box earns its price

Ask for any of these on the quote; we will tell you what suits the artwork, the board and the run size.

  • Foil stamping
    Metallic or coloured foil, flat or patterned.
  • Embossing
    Raised or debossed relief — with or without foil.
  • Spot UV
    Gloss highlights over a matt or soft-touch base.
  • Soft-touch lamination
    Velvet hand-feel; the most-requested finish on gift boxes.

Also available

  • Matt lamination
  • Gloss lamination
  • Anti-scratch lamination
  • Plastic-free finish
  • Starlight lamination (fine glitter fleck)
  • Spot colour printing
  • Recyclable board options
Spot colour, matched to your reference. Send the reference you already work to — a printed sample, a swatch or a colour code — and we match the ink to it.
Want to feel the finishes rather than read about them? Ask for a swatch card — soft-touch, anti-scratch and plastic-free laminate on carton board — and we will post one out.
