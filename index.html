Folding cartons

Printed paperboard cartons for retail shelf and e-commerce, in the board weight and finish your product needs. Supplied flat, folded and glued.

How we work

From a dimension to a box, in five steps

  1. Send your dimensions

    Product size with any headspace you need, quantity and finishing. A sketch, a photograph or an existing dieline is fine.

  2. We draw the dieline

    Structural drawing at no charge, so the fit is checked before artwork and tooling.

  3. You get a quote

    Inside one working day, with the dieline attached so you can see what is being priced.

  4. Sample

    Typically 7–10 days; free of charge, you cover the courier. Special materials and finishes can take longer.

  5. Production

    Schedule, packing and shipping terms are confirmed with the quote before anything goes on the press.

Finishing & materials

The surface is where a box earns its price

Ask for any of these on the quote; we will tell you what suits the artwork, the board and the run size.

  • Foil stamping
    Metallic or coloured foil, flat or patterned.
  • Embossing
    Raised or debossed relief — with or without foil.
  • Spot UV
    Gloss highlights over a matt or soft-touch base.
  • Soft-touch lamination
    Velvet hand-feel; the most-requested finish on gift boxes.

Also available

  • Matt lamination
  • Gloss lamination
  • Anti-scratch lamination
  • Plastic-free lamination
  • Starlight lamination
  • Spot colour printing
  • Recyclable board options
Spot colour, matched to your reference. Send the reference you already work to — a printed sample, a swatch or a colour code — and we match the ink to it.
Want to feel the finishes rather than read about them? Ask for a swatch card — soft-touch, anti-scratch and plastic-free laminate on carton board — and we will post one out.

Where it is made

We are the manufacturer, not a trading office
